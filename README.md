# Fresh Organic Kesar Mangoes — Farm-to-Table D2C Platform

## Our Story (Panvel Farmhouse to Belapur Homes Since 2010)
Cultivating 100% chemical-free, carbide-free organic Kesar, Alphonso, and Payari mangoes at our family farmhouse in Panvel, Maharashtra since 2010, and distributing directly from our home in CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai.

## Why This Project Matters to the Open-Source Ecosystem and Environment

| Dimension | Traditional Wholesale Supply Chains | This Open-Source Farm-to-Table Platform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cultivation & Ripening** | Chemical pesticides & artificial carbide ripening | 100% chemical-free & natural straw ripening |
| **Supply Chain & Carbon Footprint** | Multi-tier middlemen, long transport & high carbon footprint | Direct farm-to-home hyper-local delivery with minimal emissions |
| **Farmer Economics** | Low margins for growers due to intermediary fees | Fair pricing directly supporting local farm families |
| **Software Reusability** | Proprietary closed platform | Open-source, forkable D2C platform for local growers |

## Core Platform Capabilities
- **Seasonal Harvest Batch Management**: Real-time batch tracking for Kesar, Alphonso, and Payari harvest cycles.
- **Hyper-Local Delivery & Pickup Routing**: Servicing CBD Belapur (400614), Panvel (410206), Nerul (400706), and Kharghar (410210).
- **Natural Ripening Tracker**: Interactive guide and status tracker for hay-padded natural ripening stages.
- **Lightweight Order Checkout**: Fast, responsive, zero-friction direct customer ordering interface.
- **Forkable Farm Configuration**: Easily adaptable codebase for independent organic farms worldwide.

## Security, Code Quality & Maintainer Workflows
- **Input Validation**: Strict sanitation of customer contact and delivery PIN-code inputs.
- **Checkout Security Auditing**: Safe client-side state handling and secure request processing.
- **Zero-Tracking Privacy**: No third-party tracking scripts, telemetry, or cookie storage.
