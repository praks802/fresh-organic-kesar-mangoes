A note on natural ripening

  1. Unripe: Keep mangoes in their grass or hay packaging at room temperature.
  2. Beginning to ripen: Watch for a change in colour and a pleasant aroma.
  3. Ready to enjoy: A ripe mango yields gently to touch. Check individual fruit before eating.

This is general storage guidance, not live harvest or ripening tracking.
